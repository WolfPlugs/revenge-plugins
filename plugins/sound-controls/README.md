# Sound Controls

Each Discord in-app sound effect has its own mute switch in the plugin settings. Incoming ringing and voice join sounds are muted by default. Switches take effect on the next sound played and persist between restarts.

Includes incoming and outgoing ringing, voice joins/leaves/moves, connection effects, mute/deafen, camera, streams, push-to-talk, and in-app messages/mentions. Classic and seasonal sound packs are supported.

Voice audio, media playback, and soundboard audio are unaffected. Android notification channels and system-managed background call notifications use the phone's notification settings.

Disabling the plugin restores normal playback.

Tap a Play button to hear a classic sound even when its mute switch is enabled. Message and mention variants have separate previews. Previews stop after five seconds, when another preview starts, or when you leave settings. Preview playback uses the phone's current sound volume and routing.
